About the Planner

The Study Planner is more than just a digital notebook — it’s a personal productivity companion built to help students organize their academic life with clarity and confidence.

🎯 Purpose: To provide structure and motivation so students can manage assignments, readings, and exam preparations without stress.

🛠 Key Features

  • 📌 Task Management: Add tasks with titles, deadlines, and categories.
  • 📊 Progress Tracking: Visual dashboards show completed vs pending tasks.
  • 💡 Motivational Feedback: Dynamic messages to keep you inspired.
  • ⚙️ Customization: Dark mode and responsive design.
  • ✔ Success Feedback: Confirmation banners with audio cues.

🌱 Benefits

  • Builds consistency in study habits.
  • Reduces stress by making deadlines visible and manageable.
  • Encourages motivation through progress visualization.
  • Helps balance different categories of academic work.

📊 Why It Matters: Studying is not just about effort — it’s about strategy. The Study Planner transforms scattered tasks into a clear roadmap, turning effort into measurable progress.

✨ In short, the Study Planner is a personal academic coach in digital form: it organizes, motivates, and tracks progress so students can focus on learning rather than worrying about logistics.
